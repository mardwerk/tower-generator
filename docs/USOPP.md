# Usopp

The first draft used invented ammunition names and generic damage, speed and range upgrades.
Only the third middle purchase changed delivery. It did not capture enough of Usopp's kit to make the three commitments distinct.

The revision uses Usopp's slingshot, exploding stars, Pop Greens, Kabuto, Sogeking and Observation Haki.
It combines techniques from different story periods into alternative Styles. It is a tower-defense adaptation, rather than a chronological power ladder.

| Third purchase | Attack change | Intended visible change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `3-x-x Gunpowder Star` | The star expires at first contact and creates a finite-capacity area explosion. Purchased pierce applies to that explosion. | Round gunpowder ammunition and an impact blast. |
| `x-3-x Green Star: Platanus Shuriken` | Replaces the single lead star with three leaf shuriken in a spread. Each projectile has its own damage and hit capacity. | Post-timeskip hat and goggles, Black Kabuto and green leaf projectiles. |
| `x-x-3 Sogeking` | Switches to a faster piercing Kabuto shot and substantially longer targeting range. | Sogeking's mask and cape, the tall Kabuto and a distinct long-range shot. |

Later purchases develop the selected attack. Fire Star and Fire Bird Star increase the blast; Pop Green Volley and Pop Green Arsenal increase leaf-shuriken throughput; Long-range Shot and Observation Haki develop the sniper.
The first two purchases remain useful crosspaths. Reload affects the selected attack, pierce affects its damaging projectile or blast, and range increases targeting and projectile reach.

The [official character page](https://one-piece.com/character/usopp/index.html), checked on 2026-10-02, confirms slingshot sniping, varied ammunition, Pop Green seeds and the Sogeking disguise.
The [Japanese character overview](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%82%A6%E3%82%BD%E3%83%83%E3%83%97), also checked on 2026-10-02, describes Gunpowder Star, Fire Star, bird-shaped Fire Bird Star, Platanus Shuriken, Kabuto and Black Kabuto, and Observation Haki.
This overview is a secondary source for technique details, rather than new evidence of a game mechanic. Observation Haki's long-range detection is shown in the Dressrosa shot at Sugar in manga chapter 758.

The fixed three/five-projectile volleys, automatic attacks, hit capacities, costs, stats and Haki-to-camo mapping are game adaptations.
Pop Green Volley, Pop Green Arsenal, Packed Ammunition, Stronger Draw, Quick Reload, Rapid Reload, Steady Aim and Long-range Shot are descriptive upgrade names, rather than claimed canon techniques.
Leaf shuriken are the selected Pop Green attack. This draft does not try to represent Usopp's whole plant arsenal.
Fire Star and Fire Bird Star use immediate explosion damage and Bomb Shooter's immunity rules.
Their flames are the intended appearance; the current mapping does not implement fire-specific damage or a burn-duration system.

The generated records use pinned BTD6 projectile models for contact explosions, spread and Crossbow shots.
The browser displays their attack identity, damage, hit capacity and blast radius. It also shows the planned Usopp appearance.
Actual Usopp art, leaf meshes, mask, cape and bird-shaped flame effects remain to be made; inherited BTD6 assets are placeholders.
This iteration establishes distinct attacks and an art direction, rather than finished character visuals.

The three roles offer understandable choices for crowd damage, projectile coverage and distant precision.
Whether players enjoy their timing, prices and power still needs a playable prototype. A complete validator score cannot answer that question.

The current research Wiki uses selected English [Usopp overview passages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usopp).
The earlier references above explain the Tower experiment; the research-only workflow now collects English evidence and needs further named-technique review.
