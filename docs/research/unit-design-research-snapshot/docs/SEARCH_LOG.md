# Search and coverage log

Initial search: 2026-09-27. Latest targeted extension: 2026-09-28. Status: substantial evidence mapping, **not an exhaustive systematic review**. The field spans game AI, procedural content generation, natural language processing, constraint acquisition, and research methods. This log records what was actually searched and what remains. A missing item in these searches is not evidence that no relevant work exists.

## Scope and screening

Included works study at least one part of the problem: gameplay unit or mechanic design, procedural game content and its evaluation, grounded understanding of fictional characters, acquisition of unspecified constraints, transfer across games, or defensible research practice. A neighboring paper belongs only when its input, output, or evaluation reveals a concrete connection to this project. The [master library](RESEARCH_LIBRARY.md) states that connection per entry.

Excluded were generic AI papers without a specific connection, visual or audio asset generation, game implementation tutorials, references inferred solely from titles, duplicate preprint and publication records, and inherited generator designs. Game storefronts, datasets, repositories, company documentation, and writing guides are labeled separately from scholarly papers. Access failures are not treated as negative evidence.

The initial game and character [corpora](CORPORA.md) supplied search vocabulary and boundary examples. The games have not yet been reverse engineered, and no character dossier has been compiled.

## Targeted extension on 2026-10-01

This pass extended coverage by citation snowballing, a targeted arXiv API sweep for recent (2025–2026) PCG/LLM/game papers, and a focused review of character-adaptation and human-evaluation literature.

- **Citation snowballing.** Ran backward and forward citation chains (via the OpenAlex API) from ten seed papers (RTSUNITS22, TDLEVELS19, MECHGEN14, GAVEL24, PCGML18, ROLELLM24, ICA23, GENCON24, MORTAR26, CONCEPT18), then recursively from every kept paper with `cited_by_count >= 5` for two rounds. The pipeline screened 4,973 unique papers (300 OpenAlex API calls; forward-call cap reached in round 2), keeping 1,800 and excluding 1,951 (1,222 borderline). The raw screening was too broad (keyword-based), so a second, stricter re-screening pass scored each entry by title/abstract keywords and filtered out non-game and low-confidence records; 1,539 unique titles survived at score >= 6. Titles already present in the library were cross-checked, leaving **1,514 previously uncatalogued titles** available for review; the most relevant ~41 were added to the library (consi25, coopen25, latent23, diverse25, spellspace25, spellforger25, gameplay25, rpgagent26, enemy26, skills26, gptgames25, ubcl26, bsp26, tdnovel22, tdwavegen22, gameplay26, multiobj25, runtime26, instruct25, cppn26, behbtsunreal26, omnic26, llmnpccmp26, fictionrag26, dream26, baijia26, questgram21, trpgpcg20, rpgcreature23, ignite23, fighting25, charhalluc26, ooc25, npcreal25, explai26, onboarding25, dynamicnpc25, mixedinit25, script25, seamless25, xgen26, genaiarch26). Full titles, venues, DOIs, and OpenAlex-sourced summaries are in `/tmp/research/snowball_results.md` and `/tmp/research/snowball_rscreen.json`; inspection depth for the added library entries is abstract-level unless noted.

- **arXiv API sweep (2025–2026).** Ran `ti:"procedural content generation"`/`ti:"game mechanics"`/`ti:"tower defense"`/`ti:"unit generation"`/`ti:"character" AND (fidelity|benchmark|understanding)`/`ti:"game design"` with `submittedDate:[20250101 TO 20261001]`. Notable additions: gameplay26 (from-gameplay-traces causal induction into VGDL rules, up to 81% preference win rate for the SCM-based two-stage method), multiobj25 (MIPCGRL, multi-objective instruction-aware PCG-RL, up to 13.8% controllability gain), runtime26 (runtime generation+validation loop with autonomous aerial/ground agents in an endless runner), zero3d25 (zero-shot 3D map generation with LLM actor–critic agents), instruct25 (language-instructed RL for level generation), cppn26 (CPPN2WFC for globally coherent WFC content), and charhalluc26 (CHARM: multicultural role-play hallucination benchmark separating boundary-awareness from boundary-compliance failures). - **Known leads analyzed.** Retrieved titles, DOIs, venues, and full abstracts for the seven previously-unanalyzed 2025–2026 leads: consi25 ("Constraint Is All You Need: Optimization-Based 3D Level Generation with LLMs," FDG 2025), coopen25 ("Procedural Content Generation for Cooperative Games—A Systematic Review," IEEE T- Games 2025), which-structural-constraints26 (which structural constraints a VQ-VAE+autoregressive generator actually enforces; a regime map Controllable/Approachable/Unresponsive), taxonomyllm26 (a taxonomy/operationalization of PCG-LLM systems and LLM roles), hdpcg26 (high-dimensional PCG with layered and temporal dimensions; playable Unity cases), creativegame26 (multi-agent iterative HTML5 game generation with mechanic-guided planning and programmatic proxy rewards), grounding-creativity26 (LLMs synthesizing Unity games conditioned on goal-pattern playables), persona_weaver26 (disentangled world-building and moral-position banks to reduce behavioral homogeneity), balancebreak26 (game-balance regression testing as boundary discovery; BBExplorer), serious-games-npcs25 (DRL game-testing agents for PCG-NPC evaluation in a serious game).

- **Character adaptation and human evaluation review.** Commissioned a focused review of (A) semantic adaptation of fictional characters into gameplay and (B) validated human-evaluation instruments for game design. The review is recorded in `/tmp/research/character_adaptation_evaluation.md`. Key findings: CHARACTERS03 explicitly names the translation slots (actions, object use, attack damage, goals, weaknesses, skill progression); NPC07 warns believability can conflict with gameplay goals; InCharacter24 gives the closest validated character-fidelity protocol (psychological-scale interviews, up to 80.7% alignment); PCGBENCH25 shows quality/diversity/controllability are problem-specific constructs; Flake & Fried (2020) sets the measurement-validity standard; Lakens (2017) implies balance/faithfulness claims are equivalence claims; GAVEL24 and MORTAR26 show automated/proxy metrics disagree with human judgment; CSI14 measures designer *support*, not design quality. No game-design-specific inter-rater-reliability validation instrument was found (searches returned mostly medical/agricultural hits). OpenAlex returned HTTP 429 persistently during this pass; discovery used Crossref + webfetch (ACL Anthology pages gave clean abstracts for five NLP/character papers).
