# Research provenance

The retained research documents are maintained in this directory.
Their scope is stated in the [research guide](README.md), their user supplied inputs in the [corpora](CORPORA.md), their scholarly evidence in the [research library](RESEARCH_LIBRARY.md), and their searches and access limits in the [search log](SEARCH_LOG.md).
These records are sufficient to locate the sources and continue the review without another project's documentation.

The initial discovery pass on 2026-09-27 used public scholarly indexes, publication pages, and existing private bibliography indexes. The private indexes contained overlapping lists of 48 and 45 papers. Their earlier checks were principally bibliographic, with limited publisher and arXiv access. They supplied identifiers to investigate; their summaries did not establish findings. Retained papers received the independent source checks and inspection depths recorded in the local library.

For traceability, those bibliography leads came from `knowledge/framework-and-design-references.md` and `knowledge/agentic-generation-research.md` in the private `mardwerk/project` repository at revision `e93875d`. This is a historical discovery record. Readers can assess retained evidence through the primary links in this repository.

Some copied product terminology and design descriptions were encountered while locating bibliography and provenance sections during the initial inventory. They were excluded from the research evidence and design input. Product implementations, prompts, schemas, architecture, outputs, and evaluation designs were not inspected in their checkout or adopted as specifications or baselines. Prior drafts also contained unresolved citation tokens; their presence was not treated as evidence.

On 2026-09-28, the repository's documentation was narrowed to research provenance. The earlier catalog of neighboring checkouts, private workstreams, issues, demonstrations, and coordination instructions remains in Git history. It is unnecessary for continuing this research. Retained findings are supported by the local source records, and the game and character corpora remain the full user supplied lists.

Future additions should extend the local library and search record using identifiable primary sources.
The repository's [contributor instructions](../../AGENTS.md) govern current work.
These research records do not select product representations, methods, evaluation choices or experimental cases.
