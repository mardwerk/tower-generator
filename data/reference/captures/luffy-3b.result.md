# Monkey D. Luffy

## 0-0-0: 0-0-0 Gum-Gum Pistol

Placement costs 200 Gold. 0-0-0 Gum-Gum Pistol is an automatic projectile attack. Every 0.95 s it fires 1 projectile at its target; each deals 1 Normal damage with pierce 1, hitting up to 1 enemy, at range 32. Normal damage can hurt every enemy property.

## Top path: Gear 3, Inflated Impact

**1-x-x Gear 3 Force** (140 Gold). Raises damage from 1 to 2 (+1).

**2-x-x Inflated Reach** (200 Gold). Raises pierce from 1 to 2 (+1).

**3-x-x Gear 3 Impact** (320 Gold). Raises damage from 2 to 5 (+3). Raises pierce from 2 to 4 (+2). Raises splash radius from 0 to 8 (+8: sets the base value to 8; purchased additions and multipliers still apply). Aims each projectile at a different detected enemy in range, the selected target first; unused shots are lost.

**4-x-x Giant Impact** (1,800 Gold). Raises damage from 5 to 10 (+5). Raises pierce from 4 to 6 (+2).

**5-x-x Colossal Reach** (15,000 Gold). Raises damage from 10 to 18 (+8). Raises pierce from 6 to 10 (+4). Raises range from 32 to 42 (+10). Raises splash radius from 8 to 12 (+4).

## Middle path: Gear 2, Accelerated Strikes

**x-1-x Gear 2 Rhythm** (100 Gold). Shortens the attack interval from 0.95 s to 0.8075 s (attacks 18% faster).

**x-2-x Accelerated Strikes** (190 Gold). Shortens the attack interval from 0.8075 s to 0.6056 s (attacks 33% faster).

**x-3-x Gear 2 Overdrive** (450 Gold). Shortens the attack interval from 0.6056 s to 0.3028 s (attacks 100% faster). Raises damage from 1 to 2 (+1).

**x-4-x Gear 2 Surge** (4,200 Gold). Adds Gear 2 Surge, this Unit's Active Ability: for 8 s it gives the purchased attack +50% damage and 54% faster attacks, so the attack deals 3 damage every 0.1968 s at range 32. It is ready on purchase, recharges 35 s after activation and cannot reactivate while active; it grants no separate attack.

**x-5-x Sustained Surge** (35,000 Gold). Raises Gear 2 Surge duration from 8 s to 13 s (+5 s). Raises Gear 2 Surge's damage bonus from +50% to +125% (+75 percentage points).

## Bottom path: Gear 4, Compressed Force

**x-x-1 Compressed Force** (140 Gold). Raises damage from 1 to 3 (+2).

**x-x-2 Extended Compression** (200 Gold). Raises range from 32 to 40 (+8).

**x-x-3 Gear 4 Compressed Blow** (1,500 Gold). Raises damage from 3 to 8 (+5). Raises range from 40 to 52 (+12). Replaces projectile delivery with instant delivery.

**x-x-4 Boundman Force** (4,200 Gold). Raises damage from 8 to 16 (+8). Raises pierce from 1 to 2 (+1).

**x-x-5 Snakeman Compression** (35,000 Gold). Raises damage from 16 to 28 (+12). Shortens the attack interval from 0.95 s to 0.665 s (attacks 43% faster). Raises range from 52 to 64 (+12).
